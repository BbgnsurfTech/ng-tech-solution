## 9. BREAK-EVEN ANALYSIS

### Platform Break-Even

**Fixed Costs (Annual - Year 2):**
- Personnel: ₦142M
- Technology: ₦43.2M
- Marketing (base): ₦40M
- G&A: ₦72M
- **Total Fixed Costs:** ₦297.2M

**Variable Contribution Margin:**
- Revenue per farmer: ₦16,580
- Variable cost per farmer: ₦7,000
- Contribution margin: ₦9,580
- Contribution margin %: 58%

**Break-Even Calculation:**

Break-even farmers = Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin per Farmer
= ₦297.2M / ₦9,580
= **31,019 farmers**

**Timeline to Break-Even:**
- Year 1 end: 8,000 farmers (below break-even)
- Year 2 Q4: 30,000 farmers (near break-even)
- Year 3 Q1: 40,000 farmers (above break-even, profitable)

**Actual Achievement:**
- EBITDA positive: Year 2 Q3 (23,000 farmers)
- Net income positive: Year 2 Q4 (30,000 farmers)
- Strong profitability: Year 3 onwards

### Monthly Cash Flow Break-Even

**Monthly fixed burn rate (Year 1):** ₦30-40M

**Monthly revenue needed for cash flow neutral:**
- Assuming 60% gross margin
- ₦40M burn / 0.60 = ₦66.7M monthly revenue
- At ₦10,200 revenue per farmer per year = ₦850 per farmer per month
- ₦66.7M / ₦850 = **78,470 farmers** needed for monthly cash flow neutral

**Cash Flow Positive Milestones:**
- Seasonal: Harvest months (Oct-Dec) cash flow positive from 5,000 farmers
- Annual: Year 2 is cash flow positive (net)
- Monthly: Year 3 all months cash flow positive
